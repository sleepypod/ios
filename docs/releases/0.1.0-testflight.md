# sleepypod 0.1.0 — TestFlight

First TestFlight build, cut from `feat/ios-design` ahead of its merge: the Claude Design redesign (dial, slider, Night & Dawn and both-sides temperature controls), Apple Health sync with a visible sync status, and the new Compare with Apple Watch page. Version 0.1.0 is the static `project.yml` value, archived by hand before `scripts/archive.sh` existed; the script would stamp 1.0.0 on this branch (the newest reachable tag) and 1.2.2+ once cut from `main`, so later builds jump to the tag-derived version.

## App Review notes

See `metadata/review_notes.txt` for the boilerplate (demo mode, no account, local network).

NEW IN 0.1.0
- Apple Health: Settings → Apple Health. Writes sleep stages, heart rate, HRV and respiratory rate; reads sleep schedule and Watch vitals. Demo mode never writes to Health.
- Compare with Apple Watch: Sleep tab → any night → "Compare with Apple Watch". In demo mode the Watch data is synthesized so the page is reachable without a Watch.

## TestFlight — What to Test

- Onboarding on a real pod: Connect finds the pod over Bonjour; Explore demo works without one.
- Temp tab: the dial, slider, Night & Dawn and both-sides controls (Settings → temperature control) all change the set point on the pod and the pod reports it back.
- Apple Health: allow writes, then Settings → Apple Health → "Sync last 7 nights now". The night view shows "Saved to Apple Health" and the Health app shows sleepypod sleep stages and vitals for your side only.
- Apple Watch: wear the Watch to bed with Sleep tracking on, allow Sleep, Heart Rate, HRV and Respiratory Rate under READ, then open the next morning's night → Compare with Apple Watch. Stages appear in both lanes; heart rate shows two lines; HRV and breathing show both averages. The Week view's Watch agreement card fills in for that night.
- Sleep → Week: the Watch agreement trend bars jump to the tapped night.
- Light and dark mode throughout.
