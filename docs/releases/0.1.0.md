### Description [4000]

```
sleepypod — local control for your sleep pod

Take control of your sleep environment without the cloud. sleepypod connects directly to a Pod 3, 4, or 5 running the open-source sleepypod-core firmware on your home network, giving you fast, private, and reliable temperature control.

TEMPERATURE CONTROL
- Adjust bed temperature with a precision dial, a slider, or a Night & Dawn card
- Set independent temperatures for each side, or link both sides
- Warm up or cool down right now with a one-off session that ends at your wake time

SMART SCHEDULING
- Create temperature curves for the perfect night
- Design custom curves with AI assistance
- Save and reuse your favorite sleep profiles

ON-DEVICE SLEEP ANALYSIS
- Sleep stages (awake, REM, core, deep) estimated on your iPhone from the pod's vitals
- Heart rate and breathing rate extracted from raw piezo signals — no cloud processing
- Sleep quality scoring with circadian-aware algorithms
- Nightly, weekly, and monthly views

APPLE HEALTH AND APPLE WATCH
- Each morning, last night's sleep stages, heart rate, HRV, and respiratory rate are written to Apple Health for your side of the bed
- Compare any night with your Apple Watch: both stage timelines side by side, where they agree and differ, heart rate as two lines, HRV and breathing averages
- Track agreement over the week

LIVE SENSOR DATA
- Real-time piezo waveform with DSP-based vitals extraction
- Bed temperature matrix with zone-level detail
- Presence detection and water level

HAPTIC ALARMS
- Vibration wake-up alarms with preset or custom patterns, per side

PRIVACY FIRST
- Data is processed and stored on your pod and your iPhone — no cloud account
- No telemetry or tracking
- Your sleep data belongs to you

REQUIREMENTS
A Pod 3, 4, or 5 with sleepypod-core installed (github.com/sleepypod/core) on the same Wi‑Fi network. The pod's stock firmware and cloud service are not supported. Tap "Explore demo" on first launch to try the full app without hardware.

sleepypod is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Eight Sleep, Inc. Eight Sleep and Pod are trademarks of Eight Sleep, Inc., used only to identify compatible hardware.
```

### Notes [4000]

```
sleepypod controls an Eight Sleep Pod mattress cover over the local Wi‑Fi network. No account or login exists anywhere in the app.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT HARDWARE
1. On first launch, tap "Explore demo". Every screen then runs on simulated data: pod status and temperature, 7 nights of sleep with vitals, schedules, sensors and settings.
2. Temp tab: turn the dial; Settings (gear, top right) → temperature control switches between dial, slider, Night & Dawn and both-sides layouts.
3. Schedule tab: the week's temperature curve, Night & Dawn card, and the AI curve designer.
4. Sleep tab: Night shows stages and vitals; tap "Compare with Apple Watch" to see the pod's stages and heart rate beside the Watch's (synthesized in demo mode). Week shows totals and Watch agreement; Month shows totals.
5. Settings → Apple Health: the permission screen. Demo mode never writes to Health.
To leave demo mode: Settings (gear, top right) → "Connect to a pod".

PERMISSIONS
- Local Network and Bonjour (_sleepypod._tcp): discovers and controls the pod on the home network. Required for a real pod; demo mode does not use it.
- Health (write sleep analysis, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate; read sleep analysis, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate): saves the night's results for the user's side of the bed and compares them with an Apple Watch. All processing is on device.

ON-DEVICE PROCESSING
Heart rate and breathing rate are extracted from the pod's raw piezo signal with Accelerate (vDSP). Sleep stages are classified on the iPhone with outlier filtering, temporal smoothing and transition constraints. Nothing is sent to a server.

The app needs an Eight Sleep Pod (3, 4, or 5) running the open-source sleepypod-core firmware (github.com/sleepypod/core). It does not use Eight Sleep's cloud or account.
```

### What to Test (test notes for the build)

```
- Onboarding on a real pod: Connect finds the pod over Bonjour; Explore demo works without one.
- Temp tab: the dial, slider, Night & Dawn and both-sides controls (Settings → temperature control) all change the set point and the pod reports it back.
- Apple Health: allow writes, then Settings → Apple Health → "Sync last 7 nights now". The night view shows "Saved to Apple Health" and the Health app shows sleepypod sleep stages and vitals for your side only.
- Apple Watch: wear the Watch to bed with Sleep tracking on, allow Sleep, Heart Rate, HRV and Respiratory Rate under READ, then open the next morning's night → Compare with Apple Watch. Both stage lanes fill, heart rate shows two lines, HRV and breathing show both averages, and the Week view's Watch agreement card gets a bar for that night.
- Sleep → Week: tapping a Watch agreement bar jumps to that night.
- Light and dark mode throughout.
```
