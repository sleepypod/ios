# sleepypod 1.3.0

Third TestFlight build and the first cut from `main`: tag `v1.3.0` at `bb81c0a`, which semantic-release created when release #55 merged. It combines the redesign that TestFlight 1.1.0 shipped from `feat/ios-design` with everything `dev` gained since: background Apple Health sync with haptics and loaders (#57), full-night Health sync that rewrites stale samples (#58), the onboarding connection fix (#53), the schedule's "At wake" end action (#51), and the swept piezo scope with a 3D thermal bed (#50). The version jumps from 1.1.1 to 1.3.0 because semantic-release continues from the `v1.2.2` tag cut on `main` in March; App Store Connect only requires it to be higher than the last upload.

Every App Store Connect field is below, ready to paste. Character limits are in brackets; counts are exact.

### Notes [4000]

```
sleepypod controls an Eight Sleep Pod mattress cover over the local Wi‑Fi network. No account or login exists anywhere in the app.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT HARDWARE
1. On first launch, tap "Explore demo". Every screen then runs on simulated data: pod status and temperature, 7 nights of sleep with vitals, schedules, sensors and settings.
2. Temp tab: turn the dial; Settings (gear, top right) → temperature control switches between dial, slider, Night & Dawn and both-sides layouts.
3. Schedule tab: the week's temperature curve, Night & Dawn card, and the AI curve designer.
4. Sleep tab: Night shows stages and vitals; tap "Compare with Apple Watch" to see the pod's stages and heart rate beside the Watch's (synthesized in demo mode). Week shows totals and Watch agreement; Month shows totals.
5. Settings → Apple Health: the permission screen. Demo mode never writes to Health.
To leave demo mode: Settings (gear, top right) → "Connect to a pod".

NEW IN 1.3.0
- Apple Health sync also runs during background app refresh (BGAppRefreshTask "com.jonathanng.sleepypod.health-sync", UIBackgroundModes "fetch"), so last night's results reach Health without opening the app. It runs only when the user has turned on Health sync, skips runs while the phone is locked, and never writes in demo mode.
- Schedule tab → "At wake": turn the pod off or keep the final temperature when the schedule ends. It appears only when the connected pod's firmware supports it, so it is not visible in demo mode.
- Sensors: a 3D bed temperature map.

PERMISSIONS
- Local Network and Bonjour (_sleepypod._tcp): discovers and controls the pod on the home network. Required for a real pod; demo mode does not use it.
- Health (write sleep analysis, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate; read sleep analysis, heart rate, HRV, respiratory rate): saves the night's results for the user's side of the bed and compares them with an Apple Watch. All processing is on device.
- Background app refresh: syncs finished nights to Health as described above.

ON-DEVICE PROCESSING
Heart rate and breathing rate are extracted from the pod's raw piezo signal with Accelerate (vDSP). Sleep stages are classified on the iPhone with outlier filtering, temporal smoothing and transition constraints. Nothing is sent to a server.

The app needs an Eight Sleep Pod (3, 4, or 5) running the open-source sleepypod-core firmware (github.com/sleepypod/core). It does not use Eight Sleep's cloud or account.
```

### What to Test (test notes for the build)

```
- Apple Health, whole nights: after updating, open the app once while connected. Nights from the last 7 days are rewritten once. In Health → Sleep, last night's stages should start at your bedtime instead of a few hours later, and in-bed sessions of 16 hours or more should be gone from the week view.
- Background sync: leave Health sync on, put the phone down overnight, and check Health in the morning without opening sleepypod.
- Schedule → At wake: switch between Turn off and Maintain temperature; the choice persists, and the pod keeps its final temperature after a Maintain schedule ends. Needs a core with schedule end actions (sleepypod-core #789).
- Sensors: the 3D thermal bed matches the bed's temperatures, and the piezo waveform sweeps without jumping.
- Onboarding: Connect, Continue and Allow Health access show a spinner and give haptic feedback; denying Local Network shows the Open Settings state.
- Light and dark mode throughout, including Settings → appearance.
```

Internal testers need no review. External testers: this build has notable changes, so it goes through Beta App Review.
