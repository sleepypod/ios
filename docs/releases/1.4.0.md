# sleepypod 1.4.0

TestFlight build cut from `main` at tag `v1.4.0` (`4a16b08`), which semantic-release created when release #81 merged. It adds resting heart rate and 5-minute HRV to Apple Health and lets you rewrite nights from a chosen date (#79). It also adds the pod movement card on the Sleep screen (#78), sending Apple Watch readings to the pod (#77), the Watch movement recorder app (#76), and opt-in snore detection (#74). Not submitted for App Store review. The listing fields are unchanged from [1.3.1](1.3.1.md).

## TestFlight

### What to Test

```
- Apple Health, resting heart rate: Settings → Apple Health, turn on Resting heart rate, then tap Allow Health access again and allow it on the sheet. After a sync, Health → Resting Heart Rate shows one reading per night from sleepypod.
- Apple Health, HRV: last night's HRV in Health has about 12 readings per hour from sleepypod instead of one per minute. Switch HRV readings to Nightly average, sync, and see one reading per night that matches the Sleep screen.
- Rewrite nights since: pick a date a few days back and rewrite. Those nights are rewritten, and nights before that date are still in Health.
- Sleep screen: the movement card shows last night's pod movement; with a Watch recording, the Watch line and r appear.
- Settings → Apple Health → Send to pod: sending the last 30 days reports the new rows the pod took.
- Snore detection is off by default; turning it on asks for the microphone and keeps only snore times.
- Light and dark mode throughout.
```

Internal testers need no review. External testers: this build has notable changes, so it goes through Beta App Review.
