# sleepypod 1.5.0

TestFlight build cut from `main` at tag `v1.5.0` (`64c2ef1`), which semantic-release created when release #85 merged. It contains the glanceable Watch recorder (#83) and plain-language Send to pod copy, which says a resend is up to date instead of showing zeros (#84). Not submitted for App Store review. The listing fields are unchanged from [1.3.1](1.3.1.md).

## TestFlight

### What to Test

```
- Apple Watch: open sleepypod on the Watch. Turn the Digital Crown to set the hours, tap Record, and see the countdown. In the morning, tap Send to iPhone, and the Watch goes back to the Record screen.
- Watch ⓘ button: the explanation and the Include raw 50 Hz toggle are there, not on the main screen.
- Settings → Apple Health → Send to pod: send twice. The second send reads "Up to date".
- Light and dark mode throughout.
```

Internal testers need no review.

## Known issue

Pod HRV written to Health reads about 1.6× Apple Watch SDNN and barely tracks it: per-reading correlation 0.17, nightly 0.33, over 26 nights on one pod (2026-10-31). It stays enabled. Once core computes beat-to-beat SDNN, a `syncVersion` bump rewrites the last 7 nights, and Rewrite nights since covers older ones.

## Verification

- 176 unit tests passed on the iOS 26 simulator. The Watch app built with warnings as errors, and its Ready, Recording and Finished screens were checked in the Apple Watch Series 12 (46 mm) simulator.
- Real pod: Send to pod verified against the pod's reference tables (left side: 513 stages, 5,214 HR, 96 HRV, 927 breathing rows).
