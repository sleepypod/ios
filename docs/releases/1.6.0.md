# sleepypod 1.6.0

TestFlight build cut from `main` at tag `v1.6.0` (`00d2813`), which semantic-release created when release #95 merged. It adds Watch bed control, an alarm takeover and one-tap Send to pod on the Watch (#93), stopping a Watch recording early (#90), Export all plus a full-screen Watch movement screen with Snore detection under it (#94), and a movement comparison limited to the span both sensors recorded (#89). Not submitted for App Store review. The listing fields are unchanged from [1.3.1](1.3.1.md).

## TestFlight

### What to Test

```
- Apple Watch, bed page (opens first): turn the Digital Crown to set the temperature, tap Turn off / Turn on, and pick a side from the person button. Try it with the iPhone locked; the iPhone's Temp screen should follow.
- Apple Watch alarm: when an alarm vibrates, the Watch shows Stop and Snooze 5 min.
- Apple Watch movement (swipe down): start a recording, tap Stop, then Send to pod. The Watch says what reached the pod, including Health readings.
- iPhone Settings → Watch movement: opens full screen. Export all shares every recording in one file. Snore detection is now on this screen.
- Light and dark mode throughout.
```

Internal testers need no review.
