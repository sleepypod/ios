# sleepypod 1.8.0

App Store and TestFlight build cut from `main` at tag `v1.8.0` (`c0502db`), which semantic-release created when release #104 merged. It answers the second 5.1.1(iv) rejection of 1.3.1: the button under the Apple Health explanation now reads Continue, and no copy before the HealthKit request says "Allow" (#103). It also sends optional Watch readings to the pod with their device and stops calling pod HRV RMSSD (#97). It is the first App Store build since 1.3.1, so it also carries everything from the 1.4.0–1.7.0 TestFlight builds: the Apple Watch app, snore detection, pod movement and resting heart rate / 5-minute HRV in Health.

### App Review Information → Notes [4000]

```
sleepypod controls a sleep pod mattress cover over the local Wi‑Fi network. No account or login exists anywhere in the app.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT HARDWARE
1. On first launch, tap "Explore demo". Every screen then runs on simulated data: pod status and temperature, 7 nights of sleep with vitals, schedules, sensors and settings.
2. Temp tab: turn the dial; Settings (gear, top right) → temperature control switches between dial, slider, Night & Dawn and both-sides layouts.
3. Schedule tab: the week's temperature curve, Night & Dawn card, and the AI curve designer.
4. Sleep tab: Night shows stages and vitals; tap "Compare with Apple Watch" to see the pod's stages and heart rate beside the Watch's (synthesized in demo mode).
5. Settings → Apple Health: the explanation screen. Its "Continue" button goes directly to the system HealthKit request. Demo mode never writes to Health.
To leave demo mode: Settings (gear, top right) → "Connect to a pod".

NEW SINCE 1.3.1
- Apple Watch app: controls the bed temperature, stops or snoozes the pod's alarm, and records wrist movement overnight, which it sends only to the paired iPhone.
- Snore detection (off by default, Settings → Snore detection): listens on the iPhone overnight and keeps only the times snoring was detected. Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
- Apple Health: resting heart rate and 5-minute HRV.

PERMISSIONS
- Local Network and Bonjour (_sleepypod._tcp): discovers and controls the pod on the home network. Required for a real pod; demo mode does not use it.
- Health: writes sleep analysis, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV and respiratory rate for the user's side of the bed; reads sleep and Apple Watch vitals to compare them with the pod. Health data stays on the iPhone unless the user turns on sending Watch readings to their own pod on the home network.
- Microphone: snore detection only, after the user turns it on. Background mode "audio" keeps detection running overnight while the screen is off; it plays no audio.
- Motion & Fitness (Apple Watch): the overnight movement recorder.
- Background app refresh: syncs finished nights to Health.

ON-DEVICE PROCESSING
Heart rate and breathing rate are extracted from the pod's raw piezo signal with Accelerate (vDSP). Sleep stages are classified on the iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server; sleepypod has no server.

The app needs a compatible sleep pod running the open-source sleepypod-core firmware (github.com/sleepypod/core lists supported hardware). It does not use the manufacturer's cloud service or account.
```

The listing, keywords, URLs and privacy answers are unchanged from [1.3.1](1.3.1.md). Watch screenshots (416×496, Apple Watch 46 mm) are `docs/images/watch-*.png` on `fix/watch-movement-entry` (#102).
